### GENERAL EVALUATION GUIDELINES

1. Zero Presentation Bias [DATA-DIRECT]: Ignore penmanship, handwriting style, or minor scratch-outs. Evaluate raw text quality only.

2. No Per-Error Math [DATA-DIRECT]: Do NOT subtract fixed points per grammatical mistake (e.g., -0.5 per spelling error). Evaluate error density holistically within performance bands.

3. Scale Discipline: The four sub-scores must sum exactly to total_score, and total_score must never exceed MAX_MARK. The sub-score ceilings for each task type are given in the task-specific sections below.

4. Structural & Constraint Hard Caps:
   - General Principle [DATA-DIRECT]: Capped holistic deductions are applied for structural breaks rather than per-error math subtraction.
   - A hard cap sets the MAXIMUM achievable total_score. Award the capped total only if the writing quality otherwise merits it; a capped script may still score below the cap.
   - Standard cap = 50% of MAX_MARK, rounded down.

   | MAX_MARK | Standard cap |
   |----------|--------------|
   | 10       | 5            |
   | 8        | 4            |
   | 7        | 3            |
   | 5        | 2            |

   - Paragraph Cap [SYLLABUS-EXTRAPOLATED]: A paragraph split into multiple sub-paragraphs → capped at 5.
   - Letter/Email Cap [SYLLABUS-EXTRAPOLATED]: Missing 3 or more structural layout parts → capped at 2.
   - Summary Cap [SYLLABUS-EXTRAPOLATED]: Exceeding 1/3rd of the original passage length OR copying direct sentences (>50% verbatim) → capped at 5.
   - Theme Cap [SYLLABUS-EXTRAPOLATED]: Reproducing the poem's lines instead of stating its theme (>50% verbatim) → capped at 4.
   - Graph/Chart Cap [SYLLABUS-EXTRAPOLATED]: Including personal opinions, moral judgments, or external facts not shown in the chart → capped at 6 (this is 60% of MAX_MARK, a deliberate exception to the standard cap).

5. Grade Ceiling / Motivation Rule [DATA-DIRECT]: Standard excellent scripts receive the middle or lower value of Band 4. Award the FULL MAX_MARK ONLY if the writing displays exceptional, mature, and creative expression.

6. Performance Bands: Bands are proportional to MAX_MARK. Use the row matching the MAX_MARK you were given.

   | MAX_MARK | Band 4 | Band 3 | Band 2 | Band 1 | Band 0 |

   | 5        | 4–5    | 3      | 2      | 1      | 0      |

   The band you report must contain the total_score you awarded.

7. Within-Band Precision Tiebreaker:
   - Band 4: award the HIGHEST value only if language mechanics show ZERO errors and structure is flawless; default to the middle value for minor slips; award the LOWEST value of the band if mechanics show multiple minor errors.
   - Bands 3 and 2: award the HIGHER value if syntax/vocabulary is elevated; default to the LOWER value if syntax is standard/basic.
   - Band 1: award the HIGHEST value of the band if a coherent attempt or discernible sentence structure exists despite heavy errors; award the LOWEST value if the script is fragmented, near-entirely unreadable, or severely corrupted.
   - Where a band contains only one value, award that value.

#### PARAGRAPH WRITING — MAX_MARK 10

• context_content_data (3): Directly addresses the prompt; zero off-topic drift or generic pre-memorized filler.
• structure_format_brevity (3): Must be written in a SINGLE unified paragraph with a clear opening topic sentence.
• language_mechanics (2): Grammatical correctness, tense consistency, subject-verb agreement, spelling precision.
• originality_comparisons_paraphrase (2): Authentic phrasing, logical sentence transitions, absence of copy-pasted guide templates.

BANDS:
[Band 4: 8–10] Single block; clear topic sentence; 100% on-topic; minor to no grammatical errors; authentic phrasing.
[Band 3: 6–7]  Single block; basic topic sentence; mostly on-topic with minor generic fluff; readable with occasional slips.
[Band 2: 4–5]  HARD CAP AT 5 if broken into sub-paragraphs. High topic drift; frequent errors; formulaic text.
[Band 1: 1–3]  Complete lack of structure; off-topic or gibberish; pervasive grammatical failure; copied from stem.
[Band 0: 0]    Blank, unattempted, or completely non-English / unintelligible.

#### SUMMARIZING A TEXT / POEM — MAX_MARK 10

• context_content_data (3): Captures central theme and primary supporting points.
• structure_format_brevity (2): Length strictly kept within 1/3rd of the original passage; single coherent block.
• language_mechanics (2): Smooth transitions, clean syntax and spelling.
• originality_comparisons_paraphrase (3): Expressed entirely in the candidate's OWN words. ZERO direct sentence copying.

Note: objectivity is scored within context_content_data — deduct there for
personal opinions, external details, examples, quotes, or conversational filler.

BANDS:
[Band 4: 8–10] Perfect conciseness (~1/3rd length); original paraphrasing; captures core theme; seamless transitions.
[Band 3: 6–7]  Captures main ideas; mostly original paraphrasing with minor text reliance; slightly exceeds length limit.
[Band 2: 4–5]  HARD CAP AT 5 if >50% verbatim or length exceeds 1/2 of original. Includes irrelevant examples/opinions.
[Band 1: 1–3]  Uncritical direct copying of random passage lines; fails to identify main theme; unreadable.
[Band 0: 0]    Blank, unattempted, or completely non-English / unintelligible.

#### GRAPH & CHART DESCRIPTION — MAX_MARK 10

• context_content_data (3): Highlights key trends, highest/lowest data points, and comparative shifts accurately against the supplied chart values.
• structure_format_brevity (3): Features an overview/lead sentence, followed by grouped analytical data. ZERO personal opinion or external facts.
• language_mechanics (2): Accurate tense control (past tense for historical data), correct percentage/unit usage, clean spelling.
• originality_comparisons_paraphrase (2): Uses precise comparative terms ("surpassed", "sharp decline", "gradual rise", "in contrast to").

STANDALONE OVERRIDE CAP:
If the candidate includes personal opinions, moral judgments, or outside facts
not explicitly present in the given chart data, total_score is HARD-CAPPED at 6
regardless of writing quality or structural fluency.

BANDS:
[Band 4: 8–10] Clear overview sentence; accurate data comparisons; highlights key peaks/troughs; excellent comparative vocabulary.
[Band 3: 6–7]  Data described accurately; basic trends covered; minor omissions in comparative language. (Cap of 6 applies if external facts/opinions present.)
[Band 2: 4–5]  Listing data line-by-line without identifying trends; inaccurate numbers/percentages; high error density.
[Band 1: 1–3]  Completely misinterprets chart data; incoherent description; severe grammatical breakdown.
[Band 0: 0]    Blank, unattempted, or completely non-English / unintelligible.

#### THEME OF A POEM — MAX_MARK 8

The candidate must state and explain the central idea of the supplied poem. This
is distinct from summarizing: the task is to identify an abstract governing idea,
not to condense the content line by line.

• context_content_data (3): Identifies the poem's central theme accurately; not a paraphrase of surface events.
• structure_format_brevity (2): Coherent single block; states the theme early, then supports it; no padding.
• language_mechanics (1): Grammatical correctness, tense consistency, spelling.
• originality_comparisons_paraphrase (2): Expressed in the candidate's own words with genuine interpretation, drawing on the poem's imagery rather than quoting it wholesale.

BANDS:
[Band 4: 7–8] Accurate abstract theme clearly stated; supported by interpretation of the poem's imagery; own words throughout; minor to no errors.
[Band 3: 5–6] Theme identified correctly but supported thinly or partly by restating lines; readable with occasional slips.
[Band 2: 3–4] HARD CAP AT 4 if >50% of the response is verbatim poem text. Confuses theme with plot summary; vague or partial identification.
[Band 1: 1–2] Fails to identify any theme; copies the poem; incoherent or unreadable.
[Band 0: 0]   Blank, unattempted, or completely non-English / unintelligible.

#### COMPLETING A STORY — MAX_MARK 7

• context_content_data (2): Smooth continuation from the provided stem; clear sequential plot development.
• structure_format_brevity (2): Reaches a satisfying POSITIVE ending with a clear constructive or moral lesson; a proper title is supplied.
• language_mechanics (1): Accurate past-tense consistency; correct dialogue punctuation where used.
• originality_comparisons_paraphrase (2): Authentic narrative, creative turns, vivid descriptive detail; not a memorised story.

BANDS:
[Band 4: 6–7] Seamless stem extension; clear plot progression; positive ending with a constructive lesson; strong past-tense control; apt title.
[Band 3: 4–5] Logical stem continuation; predictable plot; clear positive conclusion or lesson; minor tense slips.
[Band 2: 3]   Weak connection to stem; disjointed pacing; abrupt or incomplete ending; missing moral; frequent tense shifting.
[Band 1: 1–2] Fails to develop the stem; uncritical stem copying; incoherent plot; severe grammatical breakdown.
[Band 0: 0]   Blank, unattempted, or completely non-English / unintelligible.

#### INFORMAL LETTER / FORMAL EMAIL — MAX_MARK 5

• context_content_data (1): Purpose stated immediately; content directly fulfills the prompt objective.
• structure_format_brevity (2): Presence and position of core components.
  - Letter: (1) Address, (2) Date, (3) Salutation, (4) Body, (5) Complimentary Close, (6) Signature.
  - Email: (1) To, (2) From, (3) Subject Line, (4) Salutation, (5) Body, (6) Sign-off.
• language_mechanics (1): Appropriate formal/informal register maintained throughout; accurate layout punctuation.
• originality_comparisons_paraphrase (1): Logical sequence of thoughts across the body; authentic phrasing rather than a memorised template.

BANDS:
[Band 4: 4–5] All structural parts present; immediate purpose; consistent register; minor to no errors.
[Band 3: 3]   1–2 structural parts missing; purpose clearly addressed; minor tone shifts; readable syntax.
[Band 2: 2]   HARD CAP AT 2 if 3+ layout components missing. Vague body text; inappropriate tone blending; frequent errors.
[Band 1: 1]   Major layout breakdown; fails prompt purpose; unreadable body text.
[Band 0: 0]   Blank, unattempted, or completely non-English / unintelligible.
